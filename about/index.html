    About Baselyne Systems

    Helping physical AI teams shorten the path from field failure to validated update. A founder-led engineering firm that builds the data and ML tools to close that loop.

    Background

    Baselyne Systems builds the infrastructure that closes the iteration loop for physical AI teams - the gap between a production failure and the next validated update. We scope every engagement around a specific, measurable outcome - faster evaluation, cleaner failure data, safer rollout.

    The founder has built infrastructure at Meta, Isomorphic Labs (Alphabet), and Booking.com - petabyte-scale data platforms, systems handling 100M+ queries per second, ML observability, and GDPR compliance at scale.

    We apply that experience to teams bringing AI to industry - manufacturing, warehouse, AMRs, inspection - helping them move from incident to validated change with less manual work. Clean handover when that's the goal, or ongoing engineering when the pipeline needs to keep evolving with you.

    How We Work

    How we engage

    You pay for results - a working system your team can operate - not for hours.

    01

    Discovery

    1-2 weeks

    We review your architecture, talk to your team, and identify the critical gap between where you are and where you need to be.

    • Architecture review and gap analysis
    • Prioritised roadmap with milestones
    02

    Scope & Outcome

    Collaborative

    We agree on a specific outcome - for example, reduce data-to-policy cycle time from weeks to days.

    • Specific outcome with measurable success criteria
    • Proposal with timeline
    03

    Implementation

    4-10 weeks typical

    We build alongside your team. Weekly syncs, shared code repositories, and continuous documentation. The deliverable is the agreed outcome.

    • Production-ready infrastructure
    • Documentation and runbooks
    04

    Handover

    Included

    Your team can operate and extend what we built independently. Training, shadowed operations, and a support window during transition.

    • Team training and operational handover
    • Post-engagement support window
    05

    Operate

    Optional, month to month

    An optional ongoing track for teams whose pipeline keeps evolving with their fleet. We stay on to run and improve it, scoped to your iteration cycle.

    • Continuous engineering against your iteration cycle
    • Optional path into our managed platform as it matures
